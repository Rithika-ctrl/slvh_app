Privacy Policy

SLVH Smart Shop

Effective Date: May 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 12, 2026

1. Introduction

SLVH Smart Shop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Mobile phone number (used for OTP-based authentication)
  • Your name (optional, for personalisation)
  • Delivery address and pickup slot preferences
  • Payment screenshots uploaded for order verification

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device token for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Order history and cart activity
  • App usage data for analytics and performance improvement

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify your identity via OTP and maintain your session
  • To process and fulfil your orders
  • To send order status updates and promotional notifications
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase (Firestore & Firebase Storage), which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure. We use Firestore Security Rules to ensure only authorised users can access their own data.

We do not store payment card details. Payment verification is done via uploaded screenshots reviewed by our team.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share data only with:

  • Google Firebase – our backend infrastructure provider
  • Law enforcement – if required by law

6. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send you order updates and promotional offers. You may disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing notifications

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@slvhshop.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
